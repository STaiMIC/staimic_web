    <h1>STaiMIC</h1>
    <p class="lead">The Strategic Translational AI and Multiomics Innovation Center is building the multiomics research infrastructure layer for precision medicine across the Mediterranean and Middle East.</p>

    <section class="feature-list">
      <h3 class="section-heading">Four components work as one integrated ecosystem.</h3>
      <ul>
        <li><strong>The Hub</strong>: <span class="highlight-blue">multiomics research infrastructure anchored in Basilicata's EU Convergence Region.</span></li>
        <li><strong>The Programmes</strong>: one shared pipeline, activated through disease verticals as grants and partnerships land.</li>
        <li><strong>The Platform</strong>: clinical AI and validated reference datasets.</li>
        <li><strong>The STaiMIC Institute</strong>: <span class="highlight-blue">applied-AI training for practising clinicians, healthcare staff, and students.</span></li>
      </ul>
    </section>

    <p class="paragraph highlight-panel">Coalition infrastructure, four partnership pathways: research agencies, pharma and medtech, clinical networks, strategic investors.</p>
